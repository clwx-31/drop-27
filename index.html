02 / JUMP DAY

Mission
timeline.

  1. 06:30
    01

    Fuel up

    Light breakfast, water, no alcohol, no empty stomach. Check the weather message before leaving.

  2. 07:30
    02

    Check in

    Bring photo ID. Get weighed privately, sign waivers, meet instructors, and complete training.

  3. 08:30
    03

    Climb to 18K

    Gear checks, board together, then a roughly 20-minute oxygen-assisted flight to altitude.

  4. 09:00
    04

    Send it

    Exit in tandem pairs, hit 120 mph, freefall for about 90 seconds, then float under canopy.

  5. AFTER
    05

    Celebrate

    Collect the footage, take the group photo, eat, hydrate, and save the drinks until everyone lands.

Times are illustrative. Weather, aircraft, and staffing can cause delays—keep the whole day open.

05 / FIELD GUIDE

Know before
you go.

A

Wear this

  • Lace-up athletic shoes
  • Flexible pants or joggers
  • Fitted layers for altitude
  • Contacts or secure glasses
B

Leave this

  • Sandals, boots, or heels
  • Loose jewelry and valuables
  • Anything in your pockets
  • Personal action cameras
C

Expect this

  • Private weigh-in at check-in
  • Training and harness fitting
  • Individual tandem instructors
  • Possible weather delays
!

Call first if…

  • You are close to 250 lb
  • You recently had surgery
  • You take relevant medication
  • You have mobility concerns

THE HONEST BRIEFING

This is an adventure,
not an amusement ride.

Skydiving has inherent risk. Choose a USPA-affiliated drop zone, listen closely during training, disclose medical issues, and let the professionals cancel when conditions are wrong.

Weather delays are normal. A cautious no-jump decision is a sign the system is working—not a ruined day.
